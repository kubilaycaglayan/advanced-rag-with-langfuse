### Findings and limits

All ten interactive runs completed without application/provider errors and returned trace IDs and provider usage/cost metadata. This small sample does not establish a statistical quality or latency improvement. Model choices and retrieval sets vary.

The first cleaned OpenAI QA1 answer received lower citation/faithfulness/relevancy scores. In the paired repeat, both answers described an affair lasting six months, initiated by Salander, supported by the same passage. Both correctness scores were 0.9333 and faithfulness/relevancy were 1.0. The citation judge still gave baseline 1.0 and cleaned 0.5, acknowledging supporting facts before denying them in its explanation. That inconsistency is recorded; rubrics and scores were not changed. The initial relevancy drop did not recur.

QA2 correctness stayed 0.5 in all four initial runs: answers named Nikolich but omitted Martin Vanger, which the reference also requires. OpenAI citation support was 0.0 before and after cleanup. These retained quality limitations were not addressed by maintenance. Missing faithfulness values remain N/A, with underlying judge errors retained in the JSON.

Model-visible templates and tool schemas match the captured baseline. The instruction manifest before/after removing always-enabled branches also matched after ordering literal templates. Prompts were not tuned.
